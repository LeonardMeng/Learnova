knowledge point:

Knowledge point: {point_name}

Rules:
- The statement must be checkable from the notes above, and unambiguous: clearly true or clearly

- Test understanding, not recall of exact wording. One sentence.
- Set `is_true` to whether the statement is true.
- `explanation`: one or two sentences on why it is true or false.
- Write in the language of the notes.
