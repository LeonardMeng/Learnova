You are quizzing a student on their own study notes. Write ONE multiple-choice question that tests
this knowledge point:

Knowledge point: {point_name}
What the notes say about it: {point_summary}

The student's notes on it:

{notes}

Rules:
- The question must be answerable from the notes above.
- Test understanding (why, how, when, what is the difference), not recall of exact wording.
- Give exactly 4 options with exactly one correct answer. `correct_index` is its position (0–3).
- The wrong options must be plausible: common misconceptions or details that are true of
  something else. Make all options similar in length and style.
- No "all of the above", "none of the above" or joke options. Don't start options with "A)" etc.
- `explanation`: one or two sentences on why the correct option is right.
- Write in the language of the notes.
